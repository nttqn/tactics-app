Privacy Policy for Marchlords

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Marchlords ("the app") is a turn-based strategy game for Android. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information collected directly by the app

Marchlords does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. The only data the app stores itself is kept locally on your device (via Android's standard app storage) and is never transmitted anywhere by the app:

  • Your campaign progress: which missions you have won and your best day count for each.
  • The battle you are currently playing, so you can continue it later.
  • The achievements you have earned.
  • Maps you build in the map editor.
  • Your music and sound-effect on/off preferences.

This local data is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Sharing maps

When you share a map you built, the app copies a text "map code" to your device's clipboard. The code contains only the map's name and layout. You decide where to paste it. When you import a map, the app reads the clipboard only when you tap the paste button on the My maps screen. The app has no online map service, and map codes are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Advertising

Marchlords shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the game free. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. It may also use this data to personalize ads, depending on your device and account settings. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by Marchlords directly. You can review or adjust how Google uses this data for ads through your device's ad settings. Learn more at Google's Ads Privacy & Terms.

Data sharing

Marchlords does not sell your data. It does not share any data with third parties beyond Google AdMob, described above, which the advertising feature needs in order to work.

Children's privacy

Marchlords is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is provided by Google's SDK, which offers its own settings and controls for compliance with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. Check back here for the latest version.
